"Loved this place! Stayed here for my 21st with a big group, place was super tidy and staff were lovely. Each room had a water filter and ensuites, plus everything else you needed. Super safe with security and location was a quick bike everywhere. Only faults were that it was super noisy as it's on a main road, but only the top four rooms. Didn't bother us too much as we were often home late, but was quite loud in the morning. Some of the showers flooded when on and would squirt up onto the roof but otherwise the bathrooms were great and clean. Kitchen is very minimal but we weren't cooking so wasn't necessary, they had a bbq if you were anyways. Only request would be a fridge for the main area instead of in every room! Alan was a great host, both him and the staff were super quick to respond, when our water filters ran out they replaced them straight away. Couldn't fault the staff, you wouldn't know they were there but still cleaned etc which was perfect! Would recommend to big groups!"
